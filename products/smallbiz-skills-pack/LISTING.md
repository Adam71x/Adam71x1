## Title

Small Business Skills Pack for Claude: Quotes, Client Emails, Review Replies, Money Summary & SOPs

(Short version: "Claude Skills for Small Business Owners")

## Description (150 words)

Your inbox, your reviews, and your bank export already hold the answers. These five Claude skills turn them into finished paperwork in minutes, with no coding, connectors, or extra software.

- **Quote & Proposal Writer**: paste a customer inquiry and your prices, get a clean quote with options, a "not included" list, payment terms, and a follow-up message.
- **Client Welcome Sequence**: a five-email onboarding sequence and a one-page "Working with me" guide that sets boundaries politely.
- **Review Reply Writer**: human, specific replies to Google, Yelp, Etsy, and Facebook reviews, including calm answers to one-star complaints.
- **Weekly Money Summary**: upload a bank, Stripe, Square, or PayPal CSV and get money in, money out, spending by category, and items to review.
- **SOP Writer**: turn a rambling voice note into a checklist your assistant can follow.

Each skill includes templates and a worked example. Commercial license included.

## Price recommendation

**$19 one-time. Launch price $12 for the first 20 buyers. Optional "Agency license" (install for up to 10 client businesses) at $59.**

Evidence (search 2026-09-24; most prices come from search snippets, not from opening the pages, because marketplace pages were not fetched):

1. Non-developer small-business owners already pay **$29 to $32** for static business templates: "Small Business Essentials Notion Template" on Etsy at $32.00, and a "Notion Company System" (SOPs + knowledge base) at $29.
2. Claude-specific business skill bundles sell at **$47 to $97**: "Solopreneur OS for Claude" at $97 one-time (a $47 founding tier was also mentioned).
3. Developer-focused Claude skill packs sell at **$9 to $29** (see the A2 listing in `products/claude-skills-pack/LISTING.md`).
4. Etsy has an active market for freelancer email swipe files, client onboarding kits (10-in-1, 49-template bundles), and ChatGPT prompt bundles for small businesses. Prices were not visible in the snippets.

**Main risk: a free alternative from Anthropic.** "Claude for Small Business" (launched 13 May 2026) is a free Cowork plugin with 15 skills, including *proposal drafter, onboarding writer, expense categoriser, invoice chasing, and customer sentiment*. It targets owners who connect QuickBooks, HubSpot, PayPal, and similar tools. This pack must be positioned as **connector-free, paste-and-go, for solo service businesses and freelancers**, with review replies and SOP writing (not in Anthropic's list) at the front of the listing. That is why the price is $19 and not $29 to $47.
